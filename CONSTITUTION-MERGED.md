# Constitution of the Crimson Tiles Riichi Mahjong Association

(adopted [DATE TBD])

## Article I: Name

The name of this organization shall be the Crimson Tiles Riichi Mahjong Association ("Crimson Tiles," "the club"). All references to "Crimson Tiles" or "the club" in this constitution refer to the Crimson Tiles Riichi Mahjong Association.

## Article II: Purpose and Objective

### Section 2.1
Crimson Tiles is an organization at The University of Alabama for the play, study, and promotion of Japanese riichi mahjong. The club's three primary missions are:

1. **Play and community.** Host regular play meetings and social gatherings where members of all skill levels — from absolute beginner through tournament-level competitor — can play riichi mahjong together.
2. **Learning and improvement.** Develop and maintain pedagogical materials, run beginner instruction, and support members' growth as players.
3. **External engagement and member-driven projects.** Represent The University of Alabama at North American riichi mahjong tournaments and contribute to the broader riichi community through content sharing and inter-club coordination. As a secondary purpose, Crimson Tiles facilitates opportunities, guidance, and shared infrastructure for members who pursue statistical analysis, programming, and other technical projects related to riichi mahjong.

### Section 2.2
In pursuit of these missions, Crimson Tiles will work to:

- partner with other student organizations, academic departments, and external riichi clubs to further the missions above;
- contribute to the growth of the North American riichi mahjong community through tournament participation, content sharing, and inter-club coordination.

### Section 2.3
Crimson Tiles explicitly prohibits any form of gambling within club activities, at club events, or on club premises.

## Article III: Relationship

### Section 3.1
Crimson Tiles is not chartered by, and does not derive its governance from, any external organization. Although this organization has members who are students at The University of Alabama and may have University employees associated with or engaged in its operations, the University does not direct, supervise, or control the organization and is not responsible and cannot be held liable for the organization's contracts, acts, or omissions.

### Section 3.2
Crimson Tiles may participate in non-governing affiliations, certifications, and registrations with external riichi mahjong organizations and bodies, including but not limited to:

- listing in the North American Riichi League registry at portal.nariichi.org;
- registration as a club with the American Riichi Association (ARA) for the purpose of hosting ARA-certified tournaments and contributing to World Riichi League ranking-eligible play;
- coordination with other riichi clubs and tournament organizers in North America.

No such affiliation, certification, or registration shall transfer governance authority over Crimson Tiles, modify this constitution, or constitute a chartering relationship.

### Section 3.3
Crimson Tiles shall comply with all objectives, rules, regulations, and policies established by The University of Alabama, including but not limited to the Code of Student Conduct, the University's hazing prevention policy, and the registration requirements of the Office of Student Involvement, and shall be responsible to the appropriate University authorities.

## Article IV: Membership

### Section 4.1
Active membership in Crimson Tiles shall be limited to students who are currently enrolled at The University of Alabama. Active members are eligible to vote and to hold office in Crimson Tiles.

### Section 4.2
Associate membership in Crimson Tiles shall be open to:

- faculty and staff of The University of Alabama;
- alumni of The University of Alabama;
- other interested community members who are not currently enrolled UA students.

Associate members may participate in all meetings and activities but are not eligible to vote or hold office.

### Section 4.3
The number of active members in the organization must equal at least one more than fifty percent (50%) of the total membership of Crimson Tiles, in compliance with The University of Alabama's student-organization composition requirement.

### Section 4.4
Persons interested in becoming active members may join after attending two (2) meetings within the same academic semester. No formal application or interview is required to obtain active member status.

### Section 4.5
Crimson Tiles shall not require dues for active or associate membership. The Executive Committee may, by majority vote and with notice provided to the active membership, propose an annual dues structure for ratification by simple majority vote at a business meeting.

### Section 4.6
Membership in registered student organizations shall be open to all students of The University of Alabama, without regard to any federally protected class, except in cases of designated fraternal organizations exempted by federal law from Title IX regulations concerning discrimination on the basis of sex.

### Section 4.7
In compliance with Alabama law (Code of Ala. § 16-1-23) and University of Alabama policy, Crimson Tiles strictly prohibits hazing in any form. For the purposes of this constitution, hazing means any intentional, knowing, or reckless act, occurring on or off University property, by one person alone or acting with others, directed against a current or prospective member, that endangers the mental or physical health or safety of that person for the purpose of joining, holding office in, or maintaining membership in Crimson Tiles. Any member found to have engaged in hazing shall be subject to removal under Section 4.8 and to any further action required by University policy and applicable law.

### Section 4.8
A member may be removed from Crimson Tiles for serious misconduct, including but not limited to hazing under Section 4.7, harassment of other members, theft or misappropriation of organization property, or actions that violate University of Alabama policies or applicable law. The procedure for member removal shall be:

1. Any member who suspects another member ("the accused member") of serious misconduct may bring the matter to an officer.
2. If the officer determines the matter substantiated by reasonable evidence, the officer shall present a removal proposal to the Executive Committee.
3. The Executive Committee shall notify the accused member of the proposal within one (1) business day. Notification may be delivered through any of the organization's official communication channels.
4. At least two (2) business days shall elapse between notification and the vote, to give the accused member the opportunity to respond. The accused member may voluntarily withdraw membership at any time before the vote is initiated.
5. The vote shall be conducted by the Executive Committee, in person or via secure online platform. Removal requires a majority vote of the Executive Committee, excluding the accused member if they are also an officer.
6. The accused member and the active membership shall be notified of the outcome within one (1) business day of the vote. A removed member shall not be refunded any dues paid for the current term, if applicable.
7. If the removed member held an officer position, that position shall be vacated immediately and filled under Article VIII.

## Article V: Organizational Leadership

### Section 5.1
The officers of Crimson Tiles shall be the President, the Vice President, the Secretary, and the Treasurer. These officers shall comprise the Executive Committee. All executive officers must be currently enrolled students at The University of Alabama.

### Section 5.2
The President shall:

- preside over all meetings of Crimson Tiles;
- serve as the primary point of contact between Crimson Tiles and The University of Alabama, including The SOURCE / Center for Student Involvement;
- assist the other officers in completing their responsibilities;
- ensure that the actions of Crimson Tiles remain consistent with this constitution and with the purpose of the organization;
- maintain registration with the Office of Student Involvement and submit any required updates to mySource.

### Section 5.3
The Vice President shall:

- plan and coordinate the regular play meetings of Crimson Tiles;
- coordinate participation in external tournaments and inter-club events;
- assume the duties of the President in the President's absence;
- oversee committee chairs appointed under Article VII.

### Section 5.4
The Secretary shall:

- keep minutes of all business meetings of Crimson Tiles;
- record attendance at business meetings and maintain the official roster of active and associate members;
- archive the official documents of the organization, including this constitution, meeting minutes, and any bylaws;
- manage the organization's official communication channels, including but not limited to the Discord server, and provide the notice required under Article IX.

### Section 5.5
The Treasurer shall:

- attend finance meetings and trainings held by the Financial Affairs Committee (FAC);
- collect, record, and deposit all funds allocated to or collected by Crimson Tiles;
- report on the financial status of the organization at each business meeting;
- plan and organize fundraising activities, if any;
- maintain all financial records and the organization's account at the financial institution named in Article X.

### Section 5.6
The terms of all officers shall be one academic year (two consecutive semesters). Officers may serve consecutive terms, with no limit on the total number of terms served.

### Section 5.7
One or more advisors shall be appointed or confirmed at the beginning of each academic year by the Executive Committee. At least one advisor must be a full-time faculty member, full-time staff member, or full-time employee of The University of Alabama, in compliance with University requirements.

### Section 5.8
The Advisor shall:

- advise Crimson Tiles to ensure compliance with University of Alabama policies and procedures;
- attend business meetings of Crimson Tiles when reasonably available, and the annual election meeting in person;
- assist in the resolution of internal disputes between members or officers.

## Article VI: Election of Officers

### Section 6.1
Crimson Tiles shall hold officer elections annually during the month of April in The University of Alabama's spring semester. The election meeting shall be open to all members. The only business considered at the election meeting shall be the election of Executive Committee members for the following academic year. The advisor must be present at the election meeting.

### Section 6.2
The President shall open the election meeting by seeking nominations for each office in the following order:

1. President
2. Vice President
3. Secretary
4. Treasurer

### Section 6.3
Any active member may nominate any active member, including themselves, to any office. All nominations must be accepted by the nominee. An individual may accept nomination for only one (1) executive officer position per election.

### Section 6.4
After nominations for each office are taken, the President will close nominations for that office. If only one individual accepts nomination, that individual will assume the position for the following academic year. If two or more individuals accept nomination, the position shall be put to a vote.

### Section 6.5
Voting shall occur by secret ballot. Ballots may be distributed in paper form at an in-person meeting or via a secure online platform designated by the Executive Committee, provided that all active members are notified of the voting platform and timeline in the meeting notice required under Section 9.5. Each active member may cast one (1) vote per office. Proxy voting is not permitted.

### Section 6.6
A simple majority of votes cast is required to elect an officer. If no candidate receives a simple majority because of a tie among two or more candidates with the highest vote count, candidates with fewer votes shall be removed and a runoff vote shall be held until one candidate receives a simple majority.

### Section 6.7
Newly elected officers shall be installed immediately following the conclusion of the election for each office. Outgoing officers shall assist incoming officers in transitioning into their roles.

## Article VII: Committees

### Section 7.1
The Executive Committee shall include the four officers of Crimson Tiles. The Executive Committee shall supervise the affairs of the organization between business meetings, make recommendations to the membership, and perform any other duties specified in this constitution.

### Section 7.2
Other committees, standing or special, may be appointed by the President or by the Executive Committee as deemed necessary to carry out the work of the organization. Anticipated standing committees include but are not limited to:

- a Tournament Committee, when Crimson Tiles is hosting an external tournament;
- a Travel Committee, to coordinate participation in external tournaments.

Committee chairs shall be active members and shall report to the Vice President.

## Article VIII: Vacancies and Removal of Officers

### Section 8.1
If, for any reason, an officer steps down or is removed from their position, the vacancy shall be filled by election of a replacement at the next business meeting, following the applicable procedures of Article VI.

### Section 8.2
In the event that the President steps down or is removed, the Vice President shall automatically assume the role of President. The vacancy in the role of Vice President shall then be filled under Section 8.1.

### Section 8.3
Officers may be removed before the expiration of their term for failure to satisfactorily perform the duties of office, for repeated absence from meetings, or for conduct improper to their office.

### Section 8.4
A motion to remove an officer may be made at any scheduled business meeting at which at least four (4) active members are present; however, the vote on such motion shall not be taken until the next scheduled business meeting at which at least four (4) active members are present. Removal requires an affirmative vote of three-fourths (3/4) of the active members present at the meeting at which the vote is taken.

### Section 8.5
A motion to remove an advisor may be made at any scheduled business meeting at which at least four (4) active members are present, and the vote may be taken at that time. The motion must include a nomination for a replacement advisor. The motion passes by simple majority (50% + 1) of active members present, provided the proposed replacement advisor accepts the nomination.

## Article IX: Meetings

### Section 9.1
At least one business meeting of Crimson Tiles shall be held per semester during the fall and spring semesters of The University of Alabama. Notice shall be provided in accordance with Section 9.5 before all business meetings.

### Section 9.2
At least one play meeting (general gathering for the playing of riichi mahjong) shall be held per week during the fall and spring semesters, provided University facilities are available and University policies permit. Play meetings shall be announced through the organization's official communication channels in a manner reasonably calculated to inform the general membership.

### Section 9.3
Crimson Tiles shall hold a separate election meeting during the month of April in The University of Alabama's spring semester, governed by Article VI. If the election meeting cannot be held during April, the election shall be held at the next business meeting.

### Section 9.4
A quorum shall be required at any meeting in order to conduct official business. Quorum shall consist of:

- at least twenty-five percent (25%) of active members for any business meeting;
- at least fifty percent (50%) of active members for the annual election meeting.

### Section 9.5
Notice of each business meeting and of the annual election meeting shall be provided at least seven (7) days in advance. Notice shall be given through whichever of the organization's official communication channels the President deems appropriate, including but not limited to email, the official Discord server, or other group-messaging platforms used by the active membership. All active members must receive notice. Notice shall include the date, time, and location of the meeting, as well as any official business that will be considered or put to a vote. A reminder notice may additionally be sent on the day of the meeting. Business items not appearing in the notice may be discussed but shall not be voted upon at that meeting.

### Section 9.6
Officers may require any individual whose conduct is materially disruptive to a meeting of Crimson Tiles to leave that meeting. Such removal shall apply only to the meeting at hand and shall not affect the individual's membership status, which is governed exclusively by Article IV. Removal from a meeting on grounds other than the safety, conduct, or order of the meeting itself is not permitted under this section.

## Article X: Finances

### Section 10.1
Crimson Tiles shall maintain a single primary account for the holding and disbursement of organization funds, at Alabama Credit Union, in the name of Crimson Tiles. The Treasurer shall be the primary signatory; the President shall be a secondary signatory.

### Section 10.2
All funds collected by or allocated to Crimson Tiles shall be deposited into the account named in Section 10.1. Crimson Tiles shall not maintain other bank or financial accounts without amendment to this constitution.

### Section 10.3
In the event that Crimson Tiles becomes defunct or is formally terminated as a registered student organization, all funds held in the account named in Section 10.1 shall be donated in full to the American Riichi Association (ARA); or, if the ARA has ceased to exist or declines the donation, to another non-profit riichi mahjong organization selected by majority vote of the active membership at the time of dissolution; or, if no such organization is available, to another registered University of Alabama student organization with a substantially similar mission, also selected by majority vote of the active membership. The account shall thereafter be officially closed.

## Article XI: Constitutional Amendments

### Section 11.1
This constitution may be amended at any business meeting of Crimson Tiles, subject to the procedures of this article.

### Section 11.2
Proposed amendments may be introduced by any active member or by the Executive Committee. Proposed amendments must be submitted to the Secretary in writing at least fourteen (14) days before the business meeting at which they will be voted upon, and the Secretary shall include the full text of each proposed amendment in the meeting notice required under Section 9.5.

### Section 11.3
Adoption of an amendment requires an affirmative vote of two-thirds (2/3) of the active members present at the meeting, provided quorum is met under Section 9.4.

### Section 11.4
An amended constitution shall take effect immediately upon adoption. The Secretary shall, within fourteen (14) days of adoption, file an updated copy of the constitution with The SOURCE / Office of Student Involvement using the Change of Constitution form available at mysource.ua.edu, in accordance with University requirements.

## Article XII: Rules of Order

### Section 12.1
The rules contained within the most recent edition of Robert's Rules of Order shall govern Crimson Tiles, to the discretion and better judgment of the Executive Committee, except where they are inconsistent with this constitution.

### Section 12.2
Words and phrases in this constitution shall be read in context and construed according to the rules of grammar and common usage. Words and phrases that have acquired a technical or particular meaning shall be construed accordingly.

### Section 12.3
If any section or portion of this constitution is ambiguous in scope, application, or intent, the matter shall be presented to the advisor, who shall recommend an interpretation. The recommended interpretation shall be binding until the constitution is amended to clarify the ambiguity, or until overturned by the Office of Student Involvement.

### Section 12.4
In the event that an issue, dispute, or situation arises that is not contemplated by this constitution, the matter shall be presented to the advisor, who shall recommend a solution or course of action. The recommendation shall be binding until the constitution is amended to provide guidance, or until overturned by the Office of Student Involvement.

### Section 12.5
If a majority of the Executive Committee disagrees with the advisor's interpretation under Section 12.3 or recommendation under Section 12.4, the matter may be presented to the Office of Student Involvement for consideration and final determination. The determination of the Office of Student Involvement shall be binding until the constitution is amended.
